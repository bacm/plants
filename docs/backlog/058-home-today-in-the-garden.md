## Problem

The dashboard lists reminders and this month's tasks. It says nothing about
what is happening in the garden now — what is in bloom, the weather, last
year's memories — and has no quick way to record anything.

## Why it matters

The home screen decides whether opening the app feels rewarding. Today it is a
to-do list; it should be a living picture of the garden with one-tap capture.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Before implementing, a mock-up is approved by the owner
- [ ] Order, top to bottom: weather or alert (when available), the capture
      action (056), grouped tasks (045), "En fleur en ce moment" with photos, a
      memory ("Il y a un an…", 029) when one exists, this month's tasks (031)
- [ ] Each section hides itself when empty; an empty garden shows 059 instead
- [ ] Fits the density goals of 053
