## Problem

Planning happens in winter and spring: the year bloom view (024) shows gaps,
but there is nowhere to note "plant a late-summer bloomer in Massif sud" or the
plants seen at a nursery.

## Why it matters

It gives a reason to open the app in the off-season and at the garden centre.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A wishlist of plants (name, optional search data, intended zone, note)
      separate from the garden
- [ ] From a gap month in the year bloom view, "Trouver une plante" opens the
      wishlist with that month as a filter hint
- [ ] "Je l'ai plantée" turns a wishlist entry into a garden plant, keeping its
      data
- [ ] Included in backups (020) with the schema test updated
